Editing

The Media, Cut and Edit pages. Clips sit on tracks under a playhead, with Resolve's keys: B for the blade, I and O to mark, J K L to play.

  • Transitions in Resolve's folders; Ctrl+T adds the standard one, and Smooth Cut hides a jump cut.
  • Titles with text and shape layers, emoji, and Write On.
  • Keyframes for zooms, slides and fades.
  • Speed changes, freeze frames, stabilising and subtitles.
  • Tracking a moving thing so a graphic follows it.
  • Files with several audio streams, such as an OBS recording, become one linked audio clip per stream.

Compositing

Every video clip can have a node graph that replaces its picture. A new one is just MediaIn to MediaOut, so nothing changes until you add nodes. The result plays on the Edit page and renders exactly as you see it.

  • Merge and Transform in full, with blend modes, apply modes and the Over, In, Held Out, Atop and XOr operators.
  • Rectangle, Ellipse, Polygon and B-Spline masks; Luma and Delta keyers; Corner Positioner; Motion Blur.
  • Spline and Keyframes panels with keyframe loops.

Color

Every clip can have a grade, a small node graph of correctors, and the timeline has a grade of its own, applied after the clips. A grade plays on the Edit page and is part of the exported video; Shift+D bypasses grades in the viewers only.

  • Lift, Gamma, Gain and Offset wheels, custom and hue curves, a qualifier, power windows.
  • LUTs in .cube format, and AgX for bright highlights.
  • Waveform, parade and other scopes.

Audio

The Audio page has track levels, meters, an equaliser and dynamics. A voice-over records straight onto the timeline from any microphone your desktop sees, through PipeWire or PulseAudio.

  • A four-band equaliser and a pitch control per clip, in the Inspector.
  • Normalise quiet and loud clips, fade and add music.

Deliver

Pick a preset such as YouTube 1080p and render. Kinoklip always exports from the original files at full quality, whatever you used for smooth playback while editing.

  • A render queue; Individual clips renders each clip to its own file.
  • Vertical video, and changing the frame rate of a timeline.
  • Subtitles burnt in or as a file; timelines to and from other editors through OpenTimelineIO.

Performance

Decoding uses the GPU's video engine (VA-API) on Intel and AMD, compositing uses plain OpenGL 3.3, and the next clip is prepared before a cut. It works with AMD, Intel and NVIDIA, integrated graphics included.

If playback still stutters, lower the playback resolution or make proxies. Neither changes the export. Fix choppy playback

Keys and Vim mode

The default keys follow DaVinci Resolve on Windows. Presets switch all of them to those of Vegas Pro, Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, Avid Media Composer, Kdenlive or Shotcut, and you can import a Premiere .kys or Final Cut .commandset file. Use the shortcuts of your old editor

Vim mode is an option, off by default, that edits with the keys of Vim or Neovim. Quick start, cheat sheet

Command line and MCP

Make a project, put a clip on its timeline, cut it and render it from a terminal or a script, without opening the window. Every command works on a project file and can print JSON. The same commands are available to an AI assistant through kinoklip mcp, a program the assistant starts on your own computer; it opens no network connection.

Privacy

No telemetry, no analytics, no automatic crash reports and no update checks. A bug report shares system information only when you choose to. The privacy page
