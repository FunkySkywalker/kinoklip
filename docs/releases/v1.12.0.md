### New
- Flatpak repository: signed stable and nightly channels, installable with `flatpak remote-add` (see the download page)
- Libadwaita (GNOME look) widget style built in, next to Kinoklip and Breeze
- Color page: Primaries Auto, Midtone Detail, Color Boost, Shadows and Highlights, and a Log palette (Shadow, Midtone, Highlight and Offset wheels with Low and High Range)
- Color page: Sat vs Lum curve, Soft Clip, and picking a colour or luma from the viewer to add curve points
- Color page: Generate 3D LUT (17, 33 or 65 point .cube) from a clip's grade, in the clips strip menu
- Scopes: CIE Chromaticity scope, and scope settings for scale and gain
- Titles: underline and strikethrough in the Title Inspector and the Text and Text3D nodes
- Welcome window: the colour scheme and the new widget style choice preview live on the whole window; Skip or closing it puts the old look back
- ARM64 AppImage

### Improved
- Switching colour scheme or widget style is about 6 times faster
- Faster startup and page switches; a hidden Inspector no longer rebuilds on every selection change
- Timeline track headers follow Resolve: lock, auto select, enable or S and M, then sync lock, with the track name under the buttons
- Drop-down lists fit their entries instead of opening taller or narrower than needed
- Timeline ruler, mixer effect and bus slots, and the Effects Library categories match Resolve's look more closely
- Builds with Qt 6.8 again (Debian 13)

### Fixed
- Voice-over count-in no longer runs long when the computer is busy
