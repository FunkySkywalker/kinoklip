### New
- Auto Align Clips: select clips on different tracks and line them up by their sound (Based on Waveform) or their timecode, from the timeline clip menu; the reference clip stays put, weak matches are reported instead of applied (issue #16)
- Auto Align from the command line and MCP: `kinoklip clip align --reference ID --clip ID --method waveform`, with an option to mute the reference's audio
- The on-screen transform controls snap the picture to the frame edges and centre lines, with alignment lines while dragging, also for pictures smaller than the frame (Snap to Guides; hold Ctrl to drag freely)
